## Simulation Contract

- **Out-of-sample construction:** folds come from `make_folds` (spec `0006`) — purged and embargoed, each test block contiguous and later in time than its training set. `fit_predict` is called once per fold on training periods only, and the resulting weights are evaluated on that fold's held-out test periods.
- **Rebalance lag:** 1 period(s), preserved within each fold.
- **Periods per year:** 252
- **Limit of the guarantee:** the harness controls fold construction, refit-per-fold, and held-out evaluation. It cannot establish that `fit_predict` did not close over global data and peek at test periods — that stays `instructions/point_in_time.md`'s concern and the `leakage` gate's.

## Data And Point-In-Time Assumptions

- **Synthetic data, disclosed.** Returns come from a seeded pseudo-random process (`random.Random(20260812)`), not a market feed. Per `instructions/data_provenance.md` this is stated rather than implied: no conclusion about any real strategy may be drawn from it.
- The artifact exists to exercise the harness end to end.
- The real point-in-time run over FRED vintages (`fred_point_in_time.py`, spec `0045`) is pending the operator's `fred_local.db`.

## Robustness Tests

- **Out-of-sample:** 5 purged, embargoed walk-forward folds; every figure above is from held-out periods.
- **Multiple testing:** these fold results must **not** be used to select among strategy variants and then reported as out-of-sample — that reintroduces p-hacking through the back door. Selecting across variants requires a deflated Sharpe correction, which this harness deliberately does not provide.
- **Capacity:** see turnover above; no capacity ceiling was modelled.
