## Boundary Check

Before returning a report, confirm it does none of the following: assess legal validity, scope of protection, infringement, or freedom to operate, or value a patent or portfolio — those are patent counsel's and the valuation owner's decisions.
If the request asked for that, redirect to the accountable human named in
this agent's `README.md` and `instructions/venture_intelligence.md`.
