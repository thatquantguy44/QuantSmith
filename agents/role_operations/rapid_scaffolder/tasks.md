# Rapid Scaffolder Tasks

## Propose Structure

Input: a new prototype idea and its rough scope.

Output: a suggested file/folder structure reusing `templates/spec/` and
`templates/data/data_contract.md`.

## Draft the Data-Contract Stub

Input: the data the prototype expects to consume.

Output: a data-contract stub with fields marked for the human to fill in
from the real source.

## Plan the Naive Baseline

Input: the prototype's goal.

Output: a naive baseline plan — what the simplest defensible first attempt
measures — with no fabricated result.

## Hand Off

Input: the completed scaffold.

Output: a named next step to `research_analyst` or `implementation`.
