# Prior-Art Scanner Tasks

## Scan Related Approaches

Input: a hypothesis or question, optionally scoped by `role_context.yml`.

Output: related approaches or prior attempts, with known strengths and
failure modes, honestly calibrated.

## Surface Open Questions

Input: the same hypothesis.

Output: a list of open questions the hypothesis raises that aren't yet
answered.

## Hand Off

Input: the scan above.

Output: a named handoff to `research_analyst` when the hypothesis warrants a
full research plan.
