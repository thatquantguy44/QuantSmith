# Lending Data Researcher Tasks

## Dataset Onboarding Research

Input: a declared lending dataset and a research context.

Output: a coverage ledger, cited findings, ranked recommendations, and a run bundle.

## Feed Incident Diagnosis

Input: a dataset that looks wrong on a business date.

Output: where missingness or rule violations concentrate (date and segment), with diagnostics.

## Point-in-Time Readiness

Input: a borrow-rate or availability-stamped dataset, an as-of, and a timezone.

Output: inverted, late-published, and not-yet-available counts, with limitations.

## Contract Proposal

Input: a completed research run.

Output: a dataset-contract proposal (observed types, ranges, grain, rule results) for review.
