# Economic Outlook Report Writer Tasks

## Write A Periodic Outlook

Input: recent and historical reads from all five upstream `economists/`
agents, the reporting period, and an as-of date.

Output: a populated `templates/docs/macro_backdrop_report.md` at outlook
cadence, filled at full depth.

## Compile A Post-Shift Outlook

Input: a material regime or scenario shift plus the reads that drove it.

Output: a fuller outlook report specifically explaining the shift, its
evidence, and its cross-asset/scenario implications.

## Identify Reporting Gaps

Input: a period where one or more upstream pillars have no fresh input.

Output: those sections explicitly marked as gaps for this period, with
what's needed to close them for the next report.
