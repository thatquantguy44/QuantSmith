# Cross-Asset Macro Linkages Tasks

## Translate A Regime Across Assets

Input: a classified macro regime.

Output: a per-asset-class read (rates, FX, credit, equities, commodities)
with stated rationale for each.

## Check Consistency Against Market Behavior

Input: a regime read plus recent price/spread behavior for one or more
asset classes.

Output: an explicit consistency/divergence assessment, naming what's
consistent and what isn't.

## Flag A Weak Linkage

Input: a regime-to-asset-class link with a historically inconsistent or
uncertain relationship.

Output: the linkage stated with its uncertainty named explicitly, not
presented with the same confidence as a stronger linkage.
